# Wireless-Solar-Car-Charging 

A smart and sustainable wireless EV charging concept powered by solar energy.

## 📌 Overview

The **Wireless Solar Car Charging Station** is an innovative electric vehicle (EV) charging concept that combines **wireless power transfer** with **solar energy**.

When an electric vehicle is positioned on the charging platform, the system can transfer electrical energy wirelessly without requiring a physical charging cable.

The charging station is powered by solar energy, making the concept more sustainable and environmentally friendly.

The project focuses on creating a **smart, automatic, cable-free, and clean-energy charging solution** for future electric vehicles.

## ⚙️ How It Works

The basic working process is:

   text
 Solar Panel
      ↓
 Charge Controller / Battery
      ↓
 Power Electronics
      ↓
 Wireless Transmitter Coil
      ↓
   ~ ~ ~ ~ ~
Wireless Power Transfer
   ~ ~ ~ ~ ~
      ↓
 Receiver Coil
      ↓
 Charging Circuit
      ↓
 Electric Vehicle Battery

 **Working Principle**

1.  The solar panel generates electrical energy from sunlight.


2.  The generated energy can be stored in a battery through a suitable charge controller.


3.  Power electronics provide the required electrical signal to the wireless transmitter.


4.  The transmitter coil creates an alternating magnetic field.


5.  A receiver coil positioned on the vehicle receives the transferred energy.


6.  The received energy is converted and regulated for charging.


7.  The vehicle can charge without a direct physical cable connection.

**⚠️ Safety & Prototype Note**

This repository presents a prototype / educational concept.

Real electric vehicles require high-power charging systems with appropriate:

Electrical isolation

Power electronics

Battery-management systems

Thermal protection

Over-current and over-voltage protection

Electromagnetic compatibility controls

Certified charging hardware


For school or science-exhibition demonstrations, a low-voltage, current-limited wireless-power setup should be used instead of directly connecting an experimental circuit to an EV battery or mains supply.
